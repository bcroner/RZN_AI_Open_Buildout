## 1. The idea in one paragraph

RZN AI, LLC is an intellectual property development and licensing firm. Its mission, in
its own words: **big problems don't scare us.** Instead of hiring a company to build
products on its technology, RZN AI opens a standing invitation. Anyone may pick up the
portfolio and build on it, **without asking permission**, by accepting one published
agreement. The money that comes back is shared the same way for everyone: **50% to the
person who built it, 40% to RZN AI, and 10% to the people.**

## 3. The split: 50 / 40 / 10

Of the **Net Receipts** from any commercial product or service built on the portfolio:

| Share | Goes to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **50%** | **The Builder**: the person or company that built and sells it | They did the building and carry the risk |
| **40%** | **RZN AI, LLC** | For the portfolio, the reference implementations, and the brand |
| **10%** | **The People's Share** | So people can pay their bills; see §5 |

**Net Receipts** means money actually received from customers for the product, minus:
refunds and chargebacks; sales and use taxes collected; payment processing fees; and the
direct cost of any physical goods sold.

**Why net, not gross.** Many businesses keep only a small share of gross revenue after
costs. A 50% royalty on *gross* revenue would leave many of them losing money on every
sale, so nobody would build. Splitting *net* receipts keeps building worthwhile.
*Decided by RZN AI on 2026-09-19: the split applies to Net Receipts as defined above.*

**The same for everyone.** Every Builder gets the same split and the same terms, with no
exclusive deals and no special rates. It's the same fair, reasonable and
non-discriminatory principle the app enforces for prices.

## 4. How a Builder opts in: no permission needed

1. **Read the published agreement** in Open Buildouts. Its fingerprint proves which
   version you accepted, and a new version never changes terms you already agreed to.
2. **Opt in.** That's the license. It's free, immediate and needs no approval.
3. **Build and sell.** Noncommercial use (study, research, hobby projects, schools,
   charities, government institutions) stays **free**, under the PolyForm Noncommercial
   license already on `getfactors` and `Robot-Stereo-Vision`. The split applies only when
   money comes in.
4. **Report and pay quarterly.** Within 30 days after each quarter, report Net Receipts
   and pay the 40% and the 10%. No receipts, nothing owed.
5. **Leave any time.** Stop selling, pay the final quarter, and you're done. No
   non-compete: you may build on anyone else's technology too.

## 5. The People's Share: who "my people" are

The 10% goes to people defined **objectively**, so it can't be steered to favorites and
nobody has to ask for it.

*Decided by RZN AI on 2026-09-19: the People's Share goes to the people who built it.*

**The rule.** Each quarter, a buildout's 10% is divided among **every contributor whose
work has ever been accepted in that buildout**, in proportion to the total they have been
paid for accepted work there. Someone whose contribution helps a product sell keeps
sharing in its success for as long as it earns, including after they stop contributing.

**Worked example.** A buildout earns $20,000 in Net Receipts in a quarter, so the People's
Share is $2,000. Over its life, Ana has been paid $600 for accepted work, Ben $300 and
Cy $100, for $1,000 in total. Ana receives $1,200, Ben $600 and Cy $200.

**When a buildout has no contributors yet,** its People's Share is held and added to the
next quarter's. If a buildout still has no accepted contributions after four quarters,
the held amount goes to `philanthropisch` for its no-strings giving, under its Charter:
no conditions, no repayment, no faith requirement.

**Keeping it fair:**
- Only work **accepted** under the Bill of Rights counts, including work accepted
  automatically when a brand didn't decide in time.
- Contributors can't be added or removed by hand. The share follows the payment records.
- Every quarter's calculation is published to that buildout's contributors, so anyone
  can check their own figure.

Whatever it adds up to, **the People's Share is a bonus, not a wage.** No one should rely on it to
pay rent until it's actually arriving.

## 6. Contributors are paid either way

Some Builders post paid work through Open Buildouts, such as writing, testing, design, or
integration. That work is covered by the **Contributor Bill of Rights**:

- It's free to join, and you can leave any time.
- There's no non-compete and no exclusivity.
- The pay rate is published before you start.
- The brand must accept or reject, with a reason, within the review window. If it doesn't, your work is accepted and payment is owed.

**The revenue split never replaces contribution pay.** A contributor is paid for accepted
work whether or not the product ever earns anything. Asking people to work now for a
share of revenue that may never come would be unpaid spec work, which is exactly what the
Bill of Rights exists to prevent.

## 7. What's not open, and why

- **Defense (State Hunter).** Weapons and defense technology are governed by U.S. export
  controls (ITAR and EAR). Opening it to anyone, anywhere, without checks could be
  illegal. It is excluded from open buildout.
- **Biotech (Syntho Bio).** Anything that reaches patients involves FDA regulation and
  clinical evidence. It is excluded until there is a regulatory plan.
- **Government uses.** RZN AI's Licensing page already offers separate royalty agreements
  for "goods and services not for sale." Those stay case by case.

## 8. Patent status

The applications above are **pending, not granted**. RZN AI's Licensing page currently
says licensing deals will be made available "upon securing patent rights." Until then:

- The Open Buildout license covers use of RZN AI's **reference implementations and
  know-how**, which RZN AI owns under copyright now.
- As patents are granted, they are **added to the same license automatically, at the
  same split**. Nobody has to renegotiate.
- **If an application ends without a patent, the split becomes 55 / 35 / 10** for work
  that relied only on it. RZN AI has less to license, so it takes less, and the Builder
  keeps the difference. *Decided by RZN AI on 2026-09-19.*

| Share | While the application is pending, or once it's granted | After it ends without a patent |
|---|---|---|
| Builder | 50% | **55%** |
| RZN AI | 40% | **35%** |
| People's Share | 10% | **10%** |

**When it applies.** An application "ends without a patent" when it is abandoned, or when
every appeal is exhausted without any claim being allowed. A routine "final rejection"
from an examiner doesn't count: it can still be answered, continued or appealed.

**What it applies to.** Only Net Receipts from a product that relies **solely** on that
application's invention. If a product also relies on another application that is still
pending or has been granted, the normal 50 / 40 / 10 continues.

**From when.** From the first full quarter after the application ends. Earlier quarters
are not recalculated.

**The People's Share never changes.** Whatever happens to a patent, the 10% for the
people stays 10%.

## 10. Questions for a lawyer before this goes live

1. **Net Receipts definition,** and audit rights for checking reported numbers.
2. **The People's Share:** tax treatment and reporting for payments to many individuals.
3. **Contributors:** independent-contractor status, especially under California's ABC test.
4. **Pending patents:** how to license applications that haven't been granted, and what
   happens if they aren't.
5. **Export controls:** confirming the defense exclusion is enough, and whether any other
   technology in the portfolio is controlled.
6. **Automatic acceptance:** making a click-to-opt-in license enforceable, including how
   the acceptance is recorded (the app stores the exact agreement fingerprint and time).
